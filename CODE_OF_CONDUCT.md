# Code of Conduct

SpecGrain is built in public and expects technical disagreement to remain respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and process without attacking people.
- State uncertainty and limitations instead of overstating conclusions.
- Welcome contributors regardless of experience level or background.
- Keep reviews actionable and proportional to the change.
- Respect licenses, attribution, privacy, and security boundaries.
- Do not use harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, or sustained disruptive behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review threads, community spaces managed by the project, and public interactions where someone is representing SpecGrain.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, restrict participation, lock threads, or block participants when necessary to protect the community. Reports should use available GitHub moderation/reporting channels and should include only the information needed to investigate the conduct concern.

Enforcement decisions should be based on observed behavior and repository evidence rather than popularity, seniority, or contribution volume.
